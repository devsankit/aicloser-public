FAQ

Got Questions?
We’ve Got Answers.

What is a sales pipeline?＋

A sales pipeline is a visual representation of your sales process. It helps you track leads and opportunities as they move through the different stages of the sales cycle.

How do I automate my sales pipeline?＋

Connect your lead sources, create custom stages, and let AI Closer automate follow-ups and reminders.

What are the benefits of using a CRM?＋

A CRM centralizes your customer data so your team can act quickly and consistently.

How do I improve lead quality?＋

Use scoring, segmentation, and clear qualification criteria to prioritize the right opportunities.

What is sales forecasting?＋

Sales forecasting estimates future revenue based on pipeline activity, deal probability, and historical performance.
